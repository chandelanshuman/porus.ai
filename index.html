Research direction

A voice belongs to someone.

Expression technology should protect agency as seriously as it pursues quality. Porus is being designed around explicit permission, reviewable decisions, provenance, and the right to withhold approval.

  • Permissionbefore synthesis
  • Reviewbefore release
  • Provenanceafter publication
  • Human authoritythroughout

These are product principles and target architecture, not a claim that every control is released today.
