A few things
worth knowing.

Can I use Porus Revoice today?

You can explore the interactive studio concept here and contact us about selected early tests. The full production workspace is still in development.

Does the studio preview generate a new dub?

No. It uses prerecorded synthetic voice samples. Script edits stay in the preview and can be downloaded; they do not regenerate the audio.

Is Porus Revoice the same as Porus AI?

Porus Revoice is the dubbing product. Porus AI is the speech-to-speech frontier foundation model at its heart.

Can we discuss a business or studio workflow?

Yes. Tell us about your content, the languages you want to reach, and your review process at hello@porus.ai.
