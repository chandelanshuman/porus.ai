## Cinematic company journey, August 2026

- Re-timed the seeded starfield so velocity begins with the horizontal human-history passage, carries behind the bright loss chapter, and returns already moving in the next-frontier section before landing in the language scene.
- Replaced the product-suite padding hover with a fine-pointer light field and subtle opposing depth motion, eliminating row reflow and preserving Studio's current-product gradient.
- Removed the rejected generated portrait, hero film, and the “Change the language. Keep the human.” campaign line.
- Restored the northern-light atmosphere, seeded faint starfield, concentrated hyperspace arrival, and scroll-pinned English to Hindi experience from the earlier concept.
- Added one real horizontal human-history passage for desktop, with touch scroll-snap and normal-flow reduced-motion alternatives.
- Rebuilt the page rhythm around lifted midnight blue, emerald and violet atmosphere, and bright editorial reading fields instead of an all-black canvas.
- Replaced generic card grids and a fictional product dashboard with typography, real audio, open workflow rows, and full-width product chapters.
- Preserved the existing `logo.svg` and `favicon.svg` artwork.
- Self-hosted Manrope and Newsreader for a distinctive interface and editorial display voice.
- Added one visibility-aware animation coordinator, responsive canvas sizing, real audio durations, decoded waveforms, exclusive playback, and a true short crossfade when the scroll passage changes language.
- Made the closed mobile menu unfocusable and gave reduced-motion visitors a static bilingual version of the central passage.
- Preserved the stronger global company narrative and explicit technical-alpha boundaries while removing unsupported availability and performance claims.
- Added `DESIGN.md` to make the visual system and future design decisions repeatable.

## Global company positioning, August 2026

- Positioned Porus.ai as a company that can grow into a suite rather than a single dubbing tool.
- Defined Porus Studio as the first proof, with API, Live, and Archive as clearly labelled future directions.
- Framed the long-term category as human expression infrastructure for a multilingual world.
- Kept the global narrative separate from the India public-mission track.

## Earlier cinematic concept

The previous single-file site introduced the liquid-glass navigation, warm-to-cool language scene, real English and Hindi WAV samples, aurora, starfield, and radial warp. The current build selectively restores those authored interaction systems without restoring unsupported claims such as broad language coverage, ready-now availability, self-hosting, or guaranteed timing and voice outcomes.
