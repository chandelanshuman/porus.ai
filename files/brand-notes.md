# Porus.ai — logo kit

## Palette (Imperial violet)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#2A1B4D` |
| Ivory (primary mark) | `#EDE9F5` |
| Old gold (accent) | `#C9A227` |
| Brass — light-background accent | `#8C6A1C` |
| Parchment — light ground | `#EFE9DB` |

## Files
- `porus-lockup-horizontal.svg` — primary. Nav, email signatures, decks.
- `porus-lockup-horizontal-light.svg` — same, for parchment or white grounds.
- `porus-crest-stacked.svg` — hero, covers, print. Motto line is optional; delete the last `<text>` to drop it.
- `porus-crown-mark.svg` / `-light.svg` — icon only, no wordmark.
- `porus-favicon.svg` — 3-point simplification with ground. Use below 40px.
- `porus-seal.svg` — certification mark, not the logo. For approved deliverables, the Trust page, sign-offs.

## Rules
- Clear space: one crown-height on all sides.
- Minimum sizes: horizontal lockup 90px wide; crown mark 24px; below 40px switch to the favicon.
- The 7-point crown never becomes 5 or 6 points. Below 24px, use the 3-point favicon instead.
- Gold is reserved for the pearls, the under-band and `.ai`. Never set the whole mark in gold.
- Never place the mark on a photograph without a solid ground behind it.
